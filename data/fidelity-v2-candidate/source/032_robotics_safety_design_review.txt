Meeting room B, 09:10. The safety review started with a video of the mobile manipulator stopping twelve centimeters before a foam obstacle. 王工暂停画面说：“这个结果看起来很安全，但我不想只看一次成功 stop。We need to know what happens when perception confidence is marginal, when the floor is reflective, and when a person moves into the corridor after the planner has already committed.”

The robot is intended for a small electronics assembly area. It carries trays between work cells and can also use a lightweight arm to place empty carriers onto a rack. The current design separates navigation and manipulation into two safety zones. When the base is moving, the arm stays folded. When the arm extends, the base speed is constrained to near zero. That logic is simple, but the review team had already found several edge cases in which “simple” was not enough.

Maya, the controls lead, opened the state diagram. “Right now, emergency stop, protective stop, and task pause are all distinct transitions. The operator UI shows them differently, but the low-level controller still funnels two of them into the same braking path.” 李工问：“那故障恢复是不是也共用？” Maya nodded. “Mostly. That’s one of the issues. If the safety scanner clears after a transient block, a protective stop can recover automatically. An emergency stop cannot. But we need to prove the transition table cannot accidentally reuse the auto-recovery path.”

The hardware team then reviewed scanner placement. The front lidar covers the main direction of travel, while side ultrasonic sensors handle close-range blind spots. A mechanical engineer pointed out that the tray itself can extend beyond the nominal chassis. “If the payload overhang changes, 机器人真正的 footprint 也变了。We cannot define the protective field only around the base.” The current software reads a payload profile before motion begins, but it does not verify the tray type after pickup. That meant a mislabeled carrier could produce an incorrect safety envelope.

Instead of immediately proposing another sensor, the team split the problem into detection and assumption management. First, the robot can check the tray fixture geometry using the wrist camera before departure. Second, the route planner can impose the larger footprint if the tray identity is uncertain. “Unknown should map to conservative, not to default,” 王工写在白板上。That phrase became one of the review actions.

At 10:00 the discussion moved to human interaction. Operators sometimes walk beside the robot because the aisle is narrow and they want to pass through a door at the same time. The nominal operating procedure says people should not escort the robot, but real behavior is different. 陈姐 from production said, “如果 SOP 跟现场习惯差太远，最后一定是 SOP 输。We should design for the behavior we actually observe.” The safety specialist agreed and proposed a low-speed shared-corridor mode with a larger side clearance, rather than assuming every person will wait behind a painted line.

The team examined logs from a pilot shift. In three cases, the robot slowed repeatedly because reflective plastic wrap created intermittent lidar returns. No unsafe motion occurred, but operators became impatient and walked closer to “help it through.” That human reaction mattered. A robot that stops too often can indirectly encourage risky workarounds. “False positives are not just productivity problems,” Maya said. “They can change human behavior around the machine.”

They next reviewed the manipulator’s force limits. The arm can place trays on a rack at shoulder height. During normal operation, torque sensing stops motion if resistance exceeds a threshold. 王工问了一个很具体的问题：“Threshold 是不是所有关节一样？” The answer was no. Each joint has a calibrated limit based on pose and expected payload. However, calibration was performed with the standard empty tray only. A tray containing misplaced components could shift the center of mass enough to alter contact forces.

The proposed mitigation was not simply to lower every threshold. Too low a limit can cause nuisance stops during normal insertion into the rack. The team instead required payload mass estimation before lifting, plus a constrained trajectory near people. The final ten centimeters of insertion would use a slower velocity and lower force envelope. If estimated payload mass fell outside the expected range, the robot would route the tray to a manual inspection point.

After lunch, the software group presented failure injection tests. They simulated a stale localization update, dropped one lidar frame out of five, delayed a safety message, and forced a timestamp jump. Most cases resulted in a controlled stop. One case was more subtle: if localization confidence dropped during a turn, the planner paused but the base controller continued the last commanded curvature for one cycle. The movement was only a few millimeters, but the safety lead rejected the behavior. “Paused means no new motion authority. 不应该靠‘距离很短’来 justify.”

That issue triggered a deeper architecture debate. The planner had been treated as the authority on whether motion should continue, but the low-level base controller also had local velocity smoothing. If the planner stopped issuing commands, smoothing could still produce residual movement. The agreed change was to add an explicit motion-permit bit generated by the safety supervisor. Velocity smoothing could operate only while that permit remained true.

At 14:20, the team reviewed maintenance mode. Technicians need to move individual joints while guards are open. The current interface requires a physical enable switch, but remote diagnostic software can also send position commands once maintenance mode is active. “Remote tool 可以看状态，但不能在 gate open 时直接 drive actuator,” 陈姐说。The group decided that local presence must be part of the authorization chain: physical enable switch held, maintenance key present, speed cap active, and remote commands limited to parameter inspection.

A cybersecurity engineer joined for the last section. He was less concerned about an attacker “taking over the robot” in a cinematic sense than about configuration drift. Safety field parameters, calibration values, and firmware versions are all files. If a routine software deployment changes one of them unintentionally, the robot can become unsafe without any dramatic compromise. The action item was to sign safety-critical configuration bundles and verify them at startup.

Before closing, 王工 read back the main decisions in mixed Chinese and English so production and engineering heard the same interpretation: “第一，unknown payload 用 conservative footprint；第二，shared corridor mode 不是 optional demo feature，而是按真实人员行为设计；第三，motion-permit 必须 bypass planner ambiguity，直接约束 low-level controller；第四，maintenance mode 需要 local presence；最后 safety config 要做 integrity verification。”

No one declared the robot “safe” at the end of the meeting. Instead, the team converted vague confidence into a list of conditions that could be tested. The design review’s value was not that everyone agreed the prototype looked cautious. It was that each assumption—payload, human behavior, residual motion, maintenance access, configuration integrity—was made explicit enough to fail on purpose before it failed on the factory floor.
